Shopper Lens

Your page. Your comparison.

Open an English desktop search on Amazon.com. Use the Shopper Lens button in the bottom right.

Defaults: Organic / unverified — Show, Sponsored placements — Dim, and Verified Amazon brands — Hide. Each control can be changed. Select up to four remaining product cards to compare their displayed information.

Organic / unverified means no sponsored disclosure or verified Amazon-brand match was detected. It is not proof that a result is organic or independent.

Restore all sets all three categories to Show. Pause on this page removes labels and controls from results until you resume. Reloading returns to the defaults above.

Only Amazon Basics and Amazon Essentials are in the ownership registry. A title mention alone stays uncertain. Unknown never means independent or unsponsored.

No account, server, analytics, storage, browsing history access, or affiliate injection. Site access is limited to Amazon.com search paths.
